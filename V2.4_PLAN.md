## 0. The daily settlement — LOCKED (single source of truth; §1–§3 are detail annexes)

At day close, judged by the day's snapshotted settings (§13):

1. Compare eaten vs **normal budget** → savings or overage. One baseline, always (audit F1).
2. **Overage** → drain bank oldest-first (§2); the uncovered rest becomes debt. A trim scheduled today is thereby missed.
3. **Savings while debt outstanding** → pay the scheduled trim (uncapped), then extra up to the daily 300 cap. Falling short of the trim = miss.
4. **Savings with debt clear** → the remainder (within the unspent part of the 300 cap) becomes a deposit, expiring in 10 days (§1).
5. **Streak** (§5): the day continues it iff it is logged and any scheduled trim was fully resolved. Debt creation alone never breaks it — it schedules (§3); only a miss or an unlogged day breaks it. (Wording corrected pre-build to match locked §5.)

A miss re-plans the outstanding total from tomorrow (§3 formula); overpayment shortens the schedule from the back. **Deposits can only exist at zero debt — by construction.**

## 1. Deposits — LOCKED (details; flow lives in §0)

- A "closed" day = in the past with ≥1 entry. Savings baseline is the **normal budget** (never the trimmed target) — see §0 step 1.
- Deposit = `min(savings remaining after §0 step 3, cap)`. Cap stays a setting, default **300**, shared between extra repayment and deposits (one daily allowance).
- Each deposit is stored with its earning date and expires **10 days** later, individually (changed from 14, Daniel 2026-08-08; hardcoded, no setting).
- Expired remainder vanishes silently and completely (not shown anywhere, per §6).
- No ceiling on total bank. Today never counts until it closes. Days with zero entries: ignored (no deposit, no debt) — Daniel always logs; no anti-exploit threshold.

## 2. Withdrawals & debt — LOCKED

- Over-budget day closes → overage is covered from deposits **oldest-first (FIFO)**.
- Whatever deposits can't cover becomes **debt** (a single outstanding number, no expiry).
- Repayment ordering and the shared 300 allowance: defined once in §0 (steps 3–4); this section adds no separate rule (audit m1).
- Bank display: positive = sum of live deposits (green); debt > 0 = shown as negative (red). By construction both can't be positive at once.

## 3. Debt repayment via budget trims — LOCKED (un-parked 2026-08-08, ships in v2.4)

- Outstanding debt gets a repayment **schedule**; on each scheduled day the target = normal budget − that day's trim.
- **Formula** (constants hardcoded: 60% · 400 · 900 · 500):
  - Day 1 trim: `clamp(60% × debt, 400, 900)`; if that covers the debt, the plan is one day.
  - Tail: remainder split evenly over `ceil(remainder / 500)` days.
  - Examples: 400 → [400] · 1,200 → [720, 480] · 2,500 → [900, 400, 400, 400, 400].
- Accounting follows §0 steps 2–3: repaid = eaten under normal budget (trim uncapped, extra capped 300); added = eaten over normal budget. Partial payments always count — money is never lost.
- **Miss** = trim not fully resolved. Streak breaks once, that day (§5). Outstanding total (unpaid + any added) gets a **fresh schedule from the formula** starting tomorrow.
- **Overpayment never re-plans** — the schedule shortens from the back / ends early.
- **Unlogged day mid-plan** = a miss: streak breaks (unlogged always does), nothing repaid, nothing added, fresh schedule from the next day. Schedules never pause or shift.
- **Repayment-day UI (the only §3 UI)**: one small muted equation line in the hero, only while a plan is active: "1,520 = 2,000 budget − 480 repayment". Nothing else anywhere.
- Trends: repayment-day bars are judged against the trimmed target — a red bar below the stepped budget line is a failed repayment day (accepted, deliberate).
- One-liner: **bad news re-plans, good news shortens.**
- Implied worst day = budget − 900 (de-facto floor — this is what replaced the deleted floor setting, 12a).

## 5. Streak rule — LOCKED

- The streak condition is defined once in §0 step 5; this section owns only display semantics (audit m1).
- **Going into debt does NOT break the streak** — it starts a §3 repayment plan. The streak breaks only at a **miss** (trim not fully resolved) or an unlogged day.
- **Optimistic display**: the streak keeps counting through debt and repayment days; it collapses only when a miss actually happens (never "broken then healed").
- Today never counts until it closes — the streak number only moves at day's end (12c: LOCKED 2026-08-08).

## 13. Settings snapshots — LOCKED (2026-08-08)

- Principle (Daniel): **everything is judged by the settings of the day**. Closed days are immutable; banked values never change retroactively.
- Each day record snapshots `{budget, cap, goal, maintenance}` when the day **closes** (protein dropped per §15): today always follows live settings (ring reacts immediately to changes); on the next app open, any unstamped past day is stamped with then-current config.
- All derived math (deposits, debt, targets, weekly closes, streak) uses the day's snapshot; repayment-day targets = day's own budget − trim (no special case — trims depend only on debt + hardcoded constants).
- Trends: budget line and goal line become **stepped lines**, stepping at change dates; untracked gaps carry the last known value forward for drawing.
- NOT snapshotted (accepted): hardcoded constants (10-day expiry; 60/400/900/500) — changed only via deliberate app updates, retroactive by design.
- Migration: trivial post-wipe; unstamped legacy days get stamped current on first v2.4 load.

## 14. Accepted quirks (named, not bugs)

- Day boundaries = device-local midnight; timezone travel can stretch/shorten a day. No machinery.
- If two devices hold different configs while offline, the same closing day can get stamped differently; day-level last-write-wins resolves it (audit n2).
- Hardcoded constants are retroactive when changed by an app update (§13).
- Editing a past day's raw entries (only possible via the data repo today) honestly recomputes that day's derived values — corrections propagate by design; snapshots still pin the settings.
